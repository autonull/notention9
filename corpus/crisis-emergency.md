# Crisis Response & Emergency Coordination Corpus

## Emergency Requests

URGENT: Need emergency shelter for family of 4 after house fire. Red Cross contacted, need immediate temporary housing.

Medical emergency: Parent needs urgent surgery, need caregiver for children (ages 3 and 6) for 2 weeks. Can compensate.

Flood evacuation: Need transportation for elderly neighbor (85) and wheelchair. Shelter at community center, 10 miles away.

Power outage: 72 hours without electricity, need generator for medical equipment (CPAP, refrigerated meds). Rural area.

Water contamination: Boil water notice for 30 days. Need bulk water delivery for household of 6, including infant formula.

## Volunteer Offers

Emergency responder offering: CERT trained, first aid/CPR certified, own transportation, available 24/7 for local disasters.

Licensed therapist offering crisis counseling: trauma-informed, grief support, free for disaster victims. Telehealth available.

Construction crew volunteering: 5 people, tools, equipment, debris removal, temporary repairs, tarping roofs. Weekend availability.

Food bank coordinator: can organize bulk food distribution, dietary restrictions, delivery to homebound. Network of 20 volunteers.

Ham radio operator: emergency communications, backup when cell towers down, ARES/RACES member. Mobile and base station.

## Community Mutual Aid

Neighborhood mutual aid network: grocery runs for immunocompromised, medication pickup, tech support, childcare exchange.

Community fridge program: 24/7 free food, donations welcome, volunteer stockers needed, located at library.

Tool lending library: power tools, gardening equipment, repair items. Membership free, donation-based maintenance.

Skill share workshops: first aid, water purification, emergency cooking, shelter building, navigation. Monthly free classes.

Carpool for medical appointments: volunteer drivers, wheelchair accessible vehicles, rural area coverage.

## Disaster Preparedness

Building 72-hour emergency kits: water, food, first aid, documents, cash, medications, pet supplies. Family of 4.

Creating family emergency plan: meeting points, out-of-state contact, evacuation routes, utility shutoff, pet plan.

Home hardening for wildfire: defensible space, ember-resistant vents, Class A roof, evacuation go-bags.

Earthquake retrofit: foundation bolting, cripple wall bracing, water heater strapping, gas shutoff valve.

Community emergency response team: training, drills, equipment cache, neighborhood captains, communication tree.

## Long-term Recovery

Post-disaster case management: FEMA applications, insurance claims, SBA loans, housing assistance, legal aid.

Mental health support groups: weekly meetings, professional facilitator, trauma processing, resilience building.

Home reconstruction coordination: contractor vetting, permit navigation, code compliance, funding sources.

Small business recovery: bridge loans, inventory replacement, customer communication, reopening planning.

Community resilience planning: hazard mitigation, infrastructure hardening, equity-centered recovery, climate adaptation.
