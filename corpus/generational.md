# Long-term Planning & Generational Corpus

## Retirement & Financial Planning

Retirement planning for early 40s: 401k max, backdoor Roth, HSA investment, taxable brokerage, real estate. Target FIRE at 55.

Estate planning for blended family: revocable trust, pour-over will, beneficiaries, guardianship, healthcare directive, POA.

College savings for newborn: 529 plan, Coverdell, UTMA, financial aid impact, grandparent contributions, investment strategy.

Long-term care insurance: hybrid life/LTC, traditional, self-fund, partnership policies, inflation protection, elimination period.

Intergenerational wealth transfer: GST trust, family limited partnership, annual gifting, tuition/medical direct pay, valuation discounts.

## Family & Caregiving

Aging parent care coordination: assisted living search, Medicaid planning, veterans benefits, sibling coordination, caregiver burnout.

Multi-generational housing: ADU construction, zoning, financing, universal design, shared expenses, privacy boundaries.

Grandparent childcare arrangement: compensation, schedule, boundaries, discipline alignment, screen time, nutrition, emergencies.

Family business succession: next generation readiness, valuation, buy-sell agreement, key person insurance, non-family management.

Special needs planning: ABLE account, special needs trust, government benefits preservation, letter of intent, trustee selection.

## Legacy & Impact

Charitable giving strategy: donor-advised fund, private foundation, charitable remainder trust, impact investing, family philanthropy.

Family mission statement: values clarification, governance structure, next generation engagement, family meetings, education.

Oral history project: interview grandparents, digitize photos, preserve recipes, document traditions, create family archive.

Mentoring next generation: financial literacy, entrepreneurship, philanthropy, governance, family history, values transmission.

Community foundation involvement: board service, grant making, donor advised funds, scholarship programs, place-based impact.

## Long-term Projects

Writing memoir/family history: interviewing relatives, research, structure, editing, self-publishing, distribution to family.

Building family compound: land acquisition, master planning, phased construction, shared amenities, governance, maintenance fund.

Creating family office: investment management, tax coordination, philanthropy, education, concierge services, staff hiring.

Establishing family scholarship: endowment sizing, criteria, selection committee, administration, donor reporting, legacy naming.

Digital legacy planning: social media, crypto, domains, photos, emails, password manager, digital executor, platform policies.

## Generational Business

Family business 100-year plan: governance constitution, family council, board independence, next-gen development, liquidity events.

Dynastic wealth strategy: dynasty trust, jurisdiction shopping, investment policy, distribution standards, trustee succession.

Family investment committee: asset allocation, manager selection, ESG integration, co-investment, direct deals, education program.
